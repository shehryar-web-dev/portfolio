The first time you wire a wallet into a web app, it feels like magic. The second
time, you notice all the edge cases. Here's the mental model that keeps it
manageable.

## The three pieces

- **Wallet adapter** — detects and connects to Phantom (and others).
- **Connection** — a client pointed at an RPC endpoint (devnet or mainnet).
- **Transactions** — built on the client, signed by the wallet, sent on-chain.

## Keep the UI honest

Users need to know what's happening at every step:

1. *Connect* — show the connected address (truncated).
2. *Sign* — make it clear the wallet popup is expected.
3. *Confirm* — wait for confirmation and show the result, with an explorer link.

## A note on networks

Develop on **devnet**, test with throwaway keypairs, and only flip to mainnet
when the flow is solid. Nothing teaches you to handle errors faster than a
failed transaction with real fees attached.
